title: "Will Private Equity Cause Another 2008 Financial Crisis?"
author: "Daniel Peng"
summary: "This essay discusses whether the recent surge in investors trying to withdraw money from major private credit funds (Blackstone, Apollo, Ares, and Blue Owl) in early 2026 signals a crisis like 2008. Drawing on Natasha Sarin's New York Times argument that it looks like a 'slow-motion bank run', it compares today's funds to the shadow banks that collapsed in 2008. It warns that roughly $300 billion in bank lending to these funds could set off a chain of failures. It also admits the comparison has limits, because private credit investors' money is locked up for years and funds can legally limit withdrawals."
