title: "A Strategic Narcotic: How Short-Term Economic Engagement Blinded the US to China’s Rare Earth Monopoly"
author: "Daniel Peng"
summary: "This article argues that US engagement policies toward China from 1979 to 2001, including the Science & Technology Cooperation Agreement, Permanent Normal Trade Relations, and the approval of the Magnequench sale, put short-term economic gains and Cold War diplomacy ahead of long-term strategic interests. Together these policies gave China access to American research, markets, and rare earth technology with little oversight. China then used that openness on purpose to build a monopoly on rare earths, which it turned into a weapon in its 2010 embargo against Japan."
