title: "Why Is Fiat Money Still Trusted?"
author: "Zijing Wu"
summary: "This essay discusses why fiat money remains trusted without gold or commodity backing. It evaluates three explanations: state authority, shared public belief, and practical necessity. Using cases of German and Zimbabwean hyperinflation and the Silicon Valley Bank collapse, it argues that no single factor alone sustains trust. These three forces work together to support fiat money, which acts as an ongoing social contract between the state and citizens."
